This Cookie Policy explains how Influead uses cookies and similar technologies on influead.in.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow websites to recognize your browser, remember information, maintain sessions, and understand how visitors interact with a website.

2. How Influead Uses Cookies

Influead may use cookies and similar technologies for the purposes below. Currently, the website stores the cookie-notice preference in your browser's local storage. Analytics and marketing cookies are not currently used. If these technologies are introduced, this policy will be updated and any required consent will be requested.

Essential Cookies

These may be necessary for:

  • Website functionality
  • Security
  • Session management
  • Form functionality
  • Payment or checkout-related functionality where applicable

These cookies may be used because they are necessary for providing requested website functionality.

Preference Cookies

These may remember choices such as:

  • Language preferences
  • Display preferences
  • Previously selected options

Analytics Cookies

Where implemented, analytics technologies may provide information about:

  • Number of visitors
  • Pages visited
  • Traffic sources
  • Website performance
  • General user interaction

Analytics information may be aggregated or otherwise processed according to the relevant provider's policies.

Marketing Cookies

Where applicable and legally permitted, marketing or advertising technologies may be used to understand campaign performance or provide relevant advertising.

Such technologies will be implemented in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

3. Third-Party Technologies

Certain website functionality may involve third-party services, including payment providers, analytics providers, hosting providers, embedded content providers, or social-media platforms.

Those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Where legally required, Influead will provide additional cookie-consent controls before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

5. Changes to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when the website, technology, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
