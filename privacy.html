Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 6 October 2026
Last Updated: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Influead handles information in connection with influead.in.

1. Information Handling

When you contact Influead by email, the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and message, is handled to provide a response. Contacting Influead opens your email application; the website does not send that message itself. Account registration and sign-in are currently unavailable.

The website stores your cookie-notice preference in your browser's local storage when you select "Got it". Your browser and service providers may also process technical information needed to load and secure the website.

2. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the website may be used to provide website functionality, remember your cookie-notice preference, maintain security, and understand or respond to communications sent directly to Influead by email.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current cookie notice stores its dismissal preference in local storage. Your browser may also store or process information needed for website functionality. For more details, see the Cookie Policy.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

Website infrastructure and third-party features may involve service providers, such as hosting and font delivery providers. Influead does not sell personal information. If additional services are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe relevant data handling.

5. Retention and Security

Cookie-notice preferences remain in your browser until you clear its local storage. Information sent directly to Influead by email is retained as reasonably needed to respond and maintain relevant records. Reasonable safeguards are used appropriate to the information and service, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. See the Security Policy.

6. Your Choices and Requests

You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. To ask a question or make a privacy-related request, contact Influead using the details below. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children to provide personal information. Please contact Influead if you believe a child has sent personal information so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as the website, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
