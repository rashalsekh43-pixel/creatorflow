Security Policy

Effective Date: 6 October 2026
Last Updated: 6 October 2026

Influead recognizes the importance of protecting personal information, business information, campaign information, and payment-related information.

This Security Policy describes the general security measures and principles applied to information handled through influead.in and Influead services.

1. Security Measures

Depending on the nature of the information and the applicable service, reasonable security measures may include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for information transmitted through supported website connections
  • Access controls
  • Authentication mechanisms
  • Restricted access to confidential information
  • Secure hosting and infrastructure practices
  • Software and dependency updates
  • Monitoring and logging where appropriate
  • Backup and recovery procedures
  • Malware and abuse protections where appropriate
  • Administrative and organizational controls

Security measures may be updated as technology and business operations develop.

2. Payment Security

Payments made through the website may be processed through Razorpay.

Influead does not intentionally store complete payment-card credentials, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, or similar payment authentication credentials on its own systems.

Payment processing is subject to the applicable security measures, terms, and privacy practices of Razorpay and the relevant payment networks or financial institutions.

Razorpay states that its payment services use security measures including PCI DSS-related controls and other security practices.

3. Access Control

Access to confidential information should be limited to persons who require such access for legitimate business purposes.

Role-based access, authentication controls, and other reasonable measures may be used to limit unauthorized access.

4. Security Incidents

If Influead becomes aware of a confirmed security incident affecting personal or confidential information, Influead will assess the incident and take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances and applicable law.

Where legally required, Influead may notify affected individuals, authorities, service providers, or other relevant parties.

5. Third-Party Services

The website and Influead services may rely on third-party providers for hosting, payments, communications, analytics, storage, security, or other infrastructure.

Influead seeks to use providers that maintain reasonable security practices appropriate to their services.

However, third-party services remain subject to their own security practices and policies.

6. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of passwords, authentication credentials, account information, and devices under their control.

You should:

  • Use strong and unique passwords
  • Avoid sharing account credentials
  • Keep devices and browsers updated
  • Avoid entering confidential information into suspicious websites
  • Notify Influead promptly if you believe your account or information has been compromised

7. Responsible Security Disclosure

If you discover a genuine security vulnerability affecting influead.in, please report it responsibly to influead@outlook.com.

Please provide enough information to support investigation and reproduction of the issue.

Do not:

  • Access, copy, modify, or delete other users' data
  • Perform denial-of-service attacks
  • Conduct destructive testing
  • Attempt social engineering against Influead personnel
  • Publish sensitive vulnerability details before Influead has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate

8. Limitations

No website, network, server, database, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Accordingly, although Influead takes reasonable measures to protect information, absolute security against every possible threat cannot be guaranteed.

9. Changes to This Security Policy

This Security Policy may be updated as infrastructure, services, or security practices evolve.

The latest version will be published on this page.
